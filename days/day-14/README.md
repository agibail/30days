Type in your grocery list and see which New York City supermarket sells it for the least. You get three answers: the cheapest single stop, the best two-stop split (and which items to buy where), and the rock-bottom total if you buy every item wherever it's cheapest. You can filter by borough and include Costco if you have a membership.

**How the prices work:** NYC chains don't publish a public price feed, so this is a price book of estimates and not live prices. It has about 80 everyday items with a typical NYC shelf price for each. That price is scaled by how each of 13 chains usually prices that kind of food: Aldi, Costco, Food Bazaar, Western Beef, Trader Joe's, Wegmans, Target, H Mart, C-Town, Key Food, Fairway, Whole Foods and Morton Williams. A few well-known outliers are set by hand, like Trader Joe's bananas and H Mart's tofu and rice. Open any item to see every store's price, follow ↗ to check the real price on that store's site, or tap a price to correct it. Corrections are saved in your browser.

The list parser understands quantities and loose names: "2 dozen eggs", "oat milk x2", "3 lb chicken thighs", "cereal", "tp".

Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS. Prices live in `data.js`.
