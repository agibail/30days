Type in an art technique and the box pulls out an index card: what you'll need, the directions, a pencilled note, and links to step-by-step guides (Instructables, wikiHow, YouTube, Wikipedia). There are 38 handwritten cards inside, filed behind manila dividers (Paint, Draw & Ink, Print, Paper, Fiber & Dye, Clay & Cast, Photo, Wax & Gold). Anything that isn't in the box gets a "found" card with a short Wikipedia description and the guide links.

Hit **File in the red box** and the card flies into the red tin in the corner. The red box keeps your saved directions in your browser, so they're still there when you come back.

Made with plain HTML/CSS/JS, Special Elite + Courier Prime + Caveat from Google Fonts, and the Wikipedia API for unknown techniques.
