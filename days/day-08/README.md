A locked black memory box you drop memories into, one index card at a time. [Open it](days/day-08/site/index.html)

- Modelled on a real black hammertone metal box: slot with a round opening in the lid, a silver key lock, and the brochure pocket on the front. Drawn in SVG, with the powder-coat texture made from noise and lighting filters.
- **Write** a memory on the ruled index card (and sign it if you like).
- **Slide it in:** press the button, ⌘/Ctrl + Enter, or grab the card and drag it up toward the slot. It tips upright, shrinks to fit, lines up over the slit, catches for a moment, then slides all the way in with a paper sound and a thunk.
- How the slide works: once the card is lined up, it moves into a layer that is clipped at the slit line, so whatever passes the slit disappears into the box.
- **Turn the key** (click the lock) to open the lid and read every card inside.
- Memories are kept in your own browser (localStorage), so each visitor has their own box.
