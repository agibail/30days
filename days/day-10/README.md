Solve an old New York Times crossword with someone else, in a beat-up newsprint crossword book.

- **Puzzles:** every Times crossword from 1976 to 2018, fetched live from the [doshea/nyt_crosswords](https://github.com/doshea/nyt_crosswords) archive. Pick a date, or grab a random one by day of the week (Monday is easiest, Saturday is hardest, Sunday is big). It opens on today's date in a random year.
- **Together:** hit *Invite a partner* and send the link. You write in graphite pencil, they write in blue ballpoint, and you each see the other's square and word highlighted live. It's peer to peer over WebRTC (PeerJS), so there's no server or account. If the host leaves, whoever's still there keeps the room open.
- **Solving:** check or reveal a square, a word or the whole puzzle, pencil in guesses, rebus squares (Esc), a shared timer, and a red SOLVED stamp. Progress is saved in the browser.
- **The look:** yellowed paper made from SVG noise, roughened page edges, a gutter shadow, a crease, a coffee ring, a dog-eared corner, highlighter for the selected word and an index card taped on for the invite.
