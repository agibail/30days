Good to know

    

      What counts as “worth a look”?
      
    
        
  • App we don't recognise: not one of the common video, browser or camera apps. Often harmless (a plugin or helper), but check you know what it is.
    • 
        
  • Late night: the camera came on between midnight and 5am.
    • 
        
  • A few seconds: on for under 10 seconds. Usually an app checking the camera works, but quick, repeated bursts are worth a second look.
    • 
        
  • Still on: the log ends with the camera running.
    • 
      

    

    

      Found something you don't recognise?
      
    
        
  • Search the app ID (the grey text under the name) to see what it belongs to.
    • 
        
  • Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Switch off anything you don't use.
    • 
        
  • Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera. Switch apps off, and check “Recent activity”.
    • 
        
  • Remove apps you don't remember installing, update your system, and change important passwords if you're worried.
    • 
      

    

    

      What this can't catch
      
This reads the record your operating system keeps. Logs roll over, so older activity may be gone (Macs often keep only a few days). Software with deep, system-level access could in theory hide from the log. A clean result is reassuring but not a guarantee. If you're seriously worried, a cheap camera cover is the one thing that's certain.
